### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

Throughout my internship, I experienced a professional, dynamic, and learning-oriented working environment. Everyone was friendly, approachable, and always willing to share their knowledge and support me whenever I encountered difficulties. This helped me integrate into the team quickly and gain confidence in completing my assigned tasks.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Administrators**

My mentor closely followed my learning progress, regularly provided constructive feedback, and guided me toward solving problems instead of simply giving me the answers. In addition, the program administrators offered timely support with documentation, schedules, and other internship-related matters, ensuring that I had a smooth and productive learning experience.

**3. Relevance Between the Internship and My Academic Major**

The internship program was closely related to the knowledge I had learned at university while also introducing me to many practical concepts that I had never experienced before. In particular, I had the opportunity to explore Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence technologies, which broadened my understanding of current trends in the Information Technology industry.

**4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development**

The program provided numerous opportunities for both technical and personal development. Besides acquiring new technical knowledge, I improved my teamwork, presentation, time management, and problem-solving skills. These are essential competencies that will benefit my future career.

**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**

I highly appreciate the positive working culture of the program. Everyone respected each other's opinions, listened attentively, and collaborated effectively to achieve common goals. The open and supportive atmosphere encouraged interns to ask questions, exchange ideas, and actively participate in discussions.

**6. Internship Benefits and Support**

The internship program was well organized with a clear learning roadmap, comprehensive learning materials, and various activities designed to support interns. In addition, the sharing sessions conducted by mentors and industry experts provided valuable insights into real working environments and career development.

**7. Personal Reflection**

The aspect I appreciated most during the internship was the opportunity to learn in a professional environment, explore new technologies, and receive dedicated guidance from my mentor. Every assignment allowed me to gain valuable practical knowledge and real-world experience.

I hope future internship programs will include more team-based practical activities or real-world project simulations so that interns can apply their knowledge more effectively. It would also be beneficial to organize networking sessions between different internship cohorts, allowing participants to exchange experiences and learn from one another.

I would definitely recommend this internship program to other students because it provides valuable exposure to a professional working environment, introduces modern technologies, and helps participants develop both technical expertise and soft skills. It is an excellent foundation for students who are interested in pursuing careers in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

**8. Suggestions and Expectations**

I hope the program can offer more career development seminars, industry-sharing sessions, and Q&A opportunities with professionals so that interns can gain a clearer understanding of career paths after completing the program.

If given the opportunity, I would be delighted to participate in future training programs or advanced internships to deepen my technical knowledge and gain more practical experience.

Finally, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the organizing team, mentors, and everyone who supported me throughout this internship. It has been a truly meaningful experience that has helped me grow not only in technical knowledge and professional skills but also in my career orientation. I sincerely hope this program will continue to develop and provide valuable learning opportunities for many more students in the future.
