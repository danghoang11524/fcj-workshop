## FCAJ-Agentic AI Build Week

During my internship, I had the opportunity to participate in an event that provided valuable experiences and introduced me to new and practical knowledge.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ-Agentic AI Build Week

&emsp;**Date and Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2027

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

&emsp;**Role:** Participant

&emsp;**Main Activities and Content:** During the event, AWS engineers introduced and demonstrated the products their teams had developed for the Hackathon competition. They shared how they came up with their ideas, designed the system architecture, and integrated AWS services with Agentic AI technologies to build their solutions. In addition, they discussed their real-world development experiences, including challenges such as limited development time, integrating multiple AWS services, debugging unexpected issues, and the approaches their teams used to overcome these obstacles.

&emsp;**Outcomes and Key Takeaways:** Through this event, I had the opportunity to explore several real-world applications built on AWS, gain a deeper understanding of the end-to-end product development process—from ideation to deployment—and learn practical approaches to solving technical challenges during software development. The event also broadened my knowledge of Agentic AI and inspired me to apply these concepts throughout my internship project.
